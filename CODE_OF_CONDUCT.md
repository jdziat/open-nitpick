# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be civil and stay on the technical merits. Disagree with the argument, not the
person making it.

Behavior that is not welcome here: personal attacks, harassment in public or in
private, insults about someone's identity or background, publishing anyone's
private information, and repeatedly pushing a settled point after a maintainer
has closed it.

This applies to issues, pull requests, code review, commit messages, and any
other space this project occupies.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close issues and pull requests, and
block accounts. A first response is usually a warning that names the specific
behavior. Repetition after a warning, or a single severe incident, ends in a
block.

## Reporting

Report a problem to hello@nocturnium.ai. Include links and enough context to
act on. Reports are read by the maintainers and are not shared further without
your agreement, except where the law requires it.

Reporting in good faith carries no risk to you, whatever the outcome.

## Attribution

This is a short, plain-language code of conduct rather than an adaptation of a
longer one. If your organization requires the Contributor Covenant, treat that
document as governing and this one as a summary of the same intent.
